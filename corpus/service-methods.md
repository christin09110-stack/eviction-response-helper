---
citation: "Cal. Code Civ. Proc. § 1011, § 1012, § 415.20"
topic: "how service affects the response deadline"
---
Personal service is effective on the day of delivery. Substituted service, where
papers are left with another adult and also mailed, is complete on the tenth day
after mailing. Service by mail alone extends the response period by five
calendar days.
