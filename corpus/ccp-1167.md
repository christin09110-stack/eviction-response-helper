---
citation: "Cal. Code Civ. Proc. § 1167"
topic: "time to respond to an unlawful detainer summons"
---
A defendant served with a summons in an unlawful detainer action must file a
written response within 10 days, excluding Saturdays, Sundays, and other
judicial holidays, after the complaint is served on the defendant. If service
is completed by mail, or in person through the Secretary of State's address
confidentiality program, the defendant has an additional five court days to
file a response. If no response is filed within that period, the plaintiff may
request entry of default.

This section was amended by AB 2347 (Stats. 2024, ch. 512), operative
1 January 2025, which extended the response period from five days to 10 days.
