---
citation: "Cal. Code Civ. Proc. § 1167"
topic: "time to respond to an unlawful detainer summons"
---
A defendant served with a summons in an unlawful detainer action must file a
written response within five days after the summons is served. Saturdays,
Sundays, and judicial holidays are excluded from this count. If the response is
not filed within that period, the plaintiff may request entry of default.
