---
citation: "Cal. Code Civ. Proc. § 1170"
topic: "answer and available defenses"
---
The defendant may answer the complaint, and may set out any defense in the
answer. Commonly raised affirmative defenses in residential unlawful detainer
matters include breach of the implied warranty of habitability, retaliatory
eviction, defective or improperly served notice, and acceptance of rent after
the notice period expired.
